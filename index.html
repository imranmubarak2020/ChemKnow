Why ChemKnow?

ChemKnow seeks to bridge this gap by connecting chemical knowledge with practical examples, guided calculations and interactive learning experiences.

Stage 1: Collection and Sorting

Used PET bottles are collected and sorted to separate PET from unwanted materials such as PVC, metals and other plastics. Proper sorting is important because contaminants can reduce the quality of recycled PET.

Scientific Insight

PET (polyethylene terephthalate) and PVC (polyvinyl chloride) are two different types of polymers. A polymer is a large molecule made up of repeating structural units.

These polymers have different chemical structures and thermal properties. PVC contamination can cause degradation during PET recycling, reducing the quality of the recovered material.

Equipment Used

  • Collection bins
  • Conveyor belts
  • Sorting equipment
Stage 2: Crushing and Washing

After sorting, PET bottles are crushed into small flakes to make further processing easier. The flakes are then washed to remove dirt, residual liquids, labels, adhesives and other impurities. This cleaning process helps prepare the PET flakes for subsequent recycling operations.

Scientific Insight

Crushing increases the exposed surface area of PET by breaking large bottles into smaller flakes. This allows washing solutions to make better contact with the material and helps remove surface contaminants.

Density-based separation can also be used during washing. PET typically sinks in water, while common floating contaminants such as polyethylene (PE) and polypropylene (PP) can be separated from it.

However, separation depends on the material's density and the conditions of the process. Not every contaminant floats, so additional sorting and cleaning methods may be necessary.

Equipment Used

  • Plastic bottle crusher
  • Washing tank
  • Friction washer
Stage 3: Drying and Flake Processing

After washing, PET flakes undergo mechanical dewatering to remove excess water. They are then dried to reduce their moisture content. Proper drying is important because moisture can cause PET to degrade during high-temperature processing. The dried flakes are prepared for further processing based on their intended application.

Scientific Insight

PET (polyethylene terephthalate) is a polyester. Its polymer chains contain ester linkages, which can be broken through a chemical reaction called hydrolysis.

When PET is heated in the presence of moisture, hydrolysis can break its polymer chains and reduce its molecular weight. This can negatively affect the mechanical properties of the recycled material.

Proper drying helps minimise this degradation before PET undergoes high-temperature processing. Industrial PET processing may use controlled drying conditions to achieve the required moisture level.

Equipment Used

  • Mechanical dewatering machine
  • Hot-air dryer
  • Storage hopper
Stage 4: Final Processing and Product Formation

Dried PET flakes are fed into an extrusion system, where they are heated until they melt. The molten material passes through a filtration system to remove remaining solid impurities. It is then pushed through a die and cooled before being cut into small recycled PET pellets. These pellets can be used as raw materials for manufacturing new products.

Scientific Insight

PET is a thermoplastic polymer. This means it can soften and melt when heated and become solid again when cooled. This property makes it suitable for processes such as extrusion and pellet production.

During extrusion, molten PET is forced through a specially shaped opening called a die. Before extrusion, melt filtration helps remove remaining solid contaminants that could affect the quality of the recycled material.

The extruded PET is cooled and cut into small pellets. These pellets can be used as raw materials for manufacturing new products, depending on their quality and intended application.

For applications requiring higher-quality PET, additional treatments such as solid-state polycondensation may be necessary to improve the polymer's molecular weight and properties.

Equipment Used

  • Extrusion machine
  • Melt filtration system
  • Pelletising machine
